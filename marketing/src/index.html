FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I get Bandwagon?

We're putting the finishing touches on the Android app and launching on Google Play soon. Check back here for the download link.

Will there be an iPhone version?

Android comes first. An iPhone version is on the roadmap for later.

Which devices work with it?

Any phone, watch, ring or scale that writes to Health Connect. If your device's app syncs there, Bandwagon can read it, with your permission.

Is it free?

Yes. Bandwagon is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking SDKs.

What can my friends see?

Only what the challenges you share use, like a step total or whether you kept a sleep pact. Heart rate, vitals, body, nutrition and sleep stages never leave your phone.

Is Bandwagon a medical app?

No. Bandwagon is a wellbeing app for staying active with friends. It doesn't diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.
