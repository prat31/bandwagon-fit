Bandwagon terms of use

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Wellbeing, not medical care

Bandwagon is a social wellbeing app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, fall detection or emergency monitoring. Always seek professional help for medical concerns and call local emergency services in an emergency.

Your account

You are responsible for the health data you choose to share and for the people you invite. Invite codes are personal; do not post them publicly. Group owners can remove members.

Fair play

Manual Health Connect entries are excluded from competitive features, and unusually high daily step totals may be held for review. We may remove members who manipulate data.

Availability

The service is provided as-is. Background sync depends on Android, device manufacturers and Health Connect, so data may be delayed. Never rely on Bandwagon for anything urgent.
