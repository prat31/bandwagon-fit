Bandwagon privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Bandwagon is an Android app for challenges, team goals and sleep pacts with friends. This policy explains what the app reads, what leaves your phone, who can see it and how to delete it.

Health Connect data we read

With your permission, Bandwagon reads these types from Android Health Connect. You choose which ones to share in Health Connect's own screen, and can change that at any time.

  • Activity: steps, distance, active calories, total calories burned, exercise sessions (workouts), floors climbed, activity intensity (active minutes) and wheelchair pushes.
  • Sleep: sleep sessions and stages.
  • Heart and vitals: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate and skin temperature.
  • Body: weight.
  • Nutrition and wellness: hydration and mindfulness sessions.

Bandwagon also asks to read in the background, so group standings and sleep pacts stay up to date about once an hour without opening the app. It does not ask for data older than Health Connect's default 30-day window.

What stays on your phone

Raw samples and individual records never leave your device. Heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, skin temperature, weight, hydration, total calories and sleep stages are only used on your phone: for the Health tab, your personal goals, and the sleep, move and recovery scores on Home. Bandwagon keeps a local cache of these daily values and scores so the app opens quickly.

What leaves your phone

At most one daily summary per day is uploaded, containing:

  • the date and your time zone;
  • daily totals for steps, distance, active calories, active minutes, workout minutes, workout count (also split by workout type, with distance), floors and mindful minutes;
  • your main sleep of the night: start time, end time and minutes asleep;
  • which data types you shared, the names of the apps that wrote the data, and whether manual entries were left out.

A field is left empty when you have not shared that type.

For your account and profile we also store:

  • from Google sign-in: your name, email address and account ID;
  • your display name, username, optional profile photo and optional "about" text;
  • an optional alias and avatar, used instead of your name in anonymous groups;
  • your time zone, settings (units, notification preferences, and birth year and sex if you enter them) and goals;
  • the groups you belong to, the challenges you take part in and the reactions you send;
  • a notification token for each device you sign in on.

Who can see it

  • Your daily summaries can only be read by you. Other members never see them directly: our servers copy only the results a feature needs (for example your step total in a step challenge, or whether you kept a sleep pact) into that group's standings.
  • Members of your groups see your display name, username and photo, or only your alias and avatar in an anonymous group, plus your challenge results and reactions in that group.
  • Other signed-in users can open your public profile (username, display name, photo and "about" text), for example from an invite you send. Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Public groups appear in Discover with their name, description, tags, photo and member count. Their members, standings and feed are visible only to members.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which screens and features are used, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (device model, Android version, app version and the stack trace). Neither contains health data. The app does not use the advertising ID and contains no advertising SDKs.

Service providers

Bandwagon runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics and Crashlytics), which processes this data on our behalf on Google Cloud servers. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Health Connect limited use

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is used only to provide Bandwagon's features described above. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending, and never transferred to anyone except as needed to run those features (the members you choose and our service provider above) or as required by law.

What we never do

We do not sell any data, use health data for advertising, or make medical or diagnostic claims. Bandwagon is not a medical device.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Access rules make sure each person can only read their own data and the groups they belong to.

Retention

  • Daily summaries: 400 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Group feed events: 90 days.
  • Profile, goals, settings and group memberships: until you delete them or your account.
  • Crash reports: 90 days, kept by Firebase Crashlytics.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing any type, or all of them, in Health Connect. Groups then show that your data is paused instead of showing zeros.
  • Download a copy of your data under You → Download my data in the app.
  • Delete your account and all of the data above in the app under You → Delete account, or without the app. Health Connect data on your phone is not touched.

Age

Bandwagon is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@bandwagon.fit.
