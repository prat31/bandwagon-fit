Bandwagon privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What we read

With your permission, Bandwagon reads steps, exercise sessions and sleep sessions from Android Health Connect. We use this to calculate challenges, team goals and sleep pacts.

What leaves your phone

Only one daily summary per day: date, time zone, step total, workout minutes, workout count and sleep duration/bedtime. Raw samples and individual records never leave the device.

Who can see it

Other members never read your daily summaries directly. Cloud Functions copy only the fields a feature needs into precomputed group standings. Sleep duration and sleep stages are never shared in Sleep Buddies.

What we never do

We do not sell health data, use it for advertising, or use it to make medical or diagnostic claims. There are no advertising SDKs in the app.

Retention and deletion

Daily summaries are kept for 400 days, feed events for 90 days and alerts for 180 days. You can delete your account and all of its data in the app under You → Delete account, or without the app.
