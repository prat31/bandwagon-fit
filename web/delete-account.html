Delete your Bandwagon account

Deleting your account removes, permanently:

  • your profile (name, photo, time zone);
  • every daily summary you uploaded (steps, workout minutes, sleep times);
  • your devices and notification tokens, and the invite codes you created;
  • your membership in every group. Groups you own pass to the next member, and groups where you were the only member are deleted with their history.

Health Connect data on your phone is not touched; that belongs to Health Connect and the apps that wrote it.

In the app (fastest)

Open Bandwagon and go to You → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email privacy@bandwagon.fit from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Bandwagon account. We confirm and delete the account and all of the data above within 30 days.
